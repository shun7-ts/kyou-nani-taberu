No account, sign-in, subscription, or demo credentials are required.

The app is localized in Japanese. It stores inventory, meal plans, shopping items and preferences locally using SwiftData and UserDefaults. There is no app-operated server, advertising SDK, external analytics, or cloud sync.

The current bundled catalog contains 244 ingredients and 192 recipes. New stock additions automatically merge compatible quantities with the same ingredient ID, storage method and expiry date/type. Expiry differences, incompatible units, and positive exact quantities mixed with unknown quantities remain separate. This preference is enabled by default and can be changed under My Page → 「食材の登録」 → 「同じ条件の在庫へ自動合算」. It does not automatically consolidate existing duplicate records.

To start: review the initial privacy explanation, turn on the acknowledgment switch, then tap 「同意して手入力から始める」. The initial inventory setup can be skipped. Use 「マイ冷蔵庫」 to add ingredients, 「ホーム」 to browse recipes, 「献立」 to choose dishes, and 「買い物」 to manage shortages and purchases. My Page is accessible from the top-left menu on Home.

Voice and receipt features are optional and have separate consent screens. Voice uses Apple's Japanese on-device speech recognition only; it does not fall back to server recognition. If the device/locale cannot provide on-device recognition or permission is denied, manual text entry remains available. A recording session is limited to 30 seconds. Receipt OCR uses Apple's Vision framework on device and accepts a selected photo or an in-app still camera capture. The app does not save original audio, original receipt images, full transcripts or full receipt text. Only reviewed, confirmed ingredient data is persisted.

Microphone and speech permissions are requested when recording starts, camera permission when opening the camera, and notification permission when opting into expiry reminders. None is needed for manual inventory entry or recipe browsing. Notifications are local and optional.

To delete all user data: Home top-left menu → 「マイページ」 → 「端末内データを全削除」, then complete the confirmation flow. Bundled ingredients and recipes remain. The app has no user account to delete.

Food images include generated illustrations/photos and licensed public images. Credits and licenses are accessible in My Page. The recipe catalog is bundled, not generated by a remote AI service.
